# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 15, 2026

Welcome to Thuthar AI.

Thuthar AI is a Mizo-language news aggregation application that provides users with access to news, AI-powered summaries, translations, and related information from trusted third-party news sources.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect
Information You Provide
When you use Thuthar AI, you may provide:

Name

Email address

Profile information associated with your Google account

Feedback, comments, or support requests

Automatically Collected Information
The App may automatically collect:

Device information

Operating system information

App usage statistics

Crash reports and diagnostics

Push notification identifiers

Advertising identifiers where permitted

2. Authentication Services
Thuthar AI uses:

Firebase Authentication

Google Sign-In

These services may collect and process information required to authenticate users and manage accounts.

3. News Content
Thuthar AI functions as a news aggregation platform.

News articles, headlines, summaries, images, publication dates, publisher information, and author information may be obtained from third-party news providers through authorized APIs, including Real-Time News Data API.

Copyright ownership of news content remains with the original publishers, authors, and copyright holders.

Where available, source attribution is displayed within the application.

4. AI Summaries and Translation
The App may use artificial intelligence and translation services to:

Generate news summaries

Translate news content into Mizo or other supported languages

Translations and summaries are generated automatically and may not always be perfectly accurate.

Users should refer to original sources for complete information.

5. Push Notifications
Thuthar AI uses OneSignal to send notifications about important news updates and application information.

Users may disable notifications at any time through device settings.

6. Advertising
The App may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy for advertising, analytics, and measurement purposes.

7. Backend Services
The App uses Supabase to store and manage application-related data and services.

8. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only:

With service providers necessary to operate the App

To comply with legal obligations

To protect users, rights, property, or security

During business transfers if required

9. Data Security
We use reasonable security measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no system can guarantee complete security.

10. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Third-Party Services
The App uses the following third-party services:

Real-Time News Data API

Firebase Authentication

Google Sign-In

Supabase

OneSignal

Google AdMob

Google Translation API

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published within the App and on the official website.
