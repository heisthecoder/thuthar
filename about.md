# About

Welcome to Thuthar AI.

Thuthar AI is a news aggregation platform that delivers news from multiple trusted sources around the world. Our mission is to provide readers with timely, accessible, and reliable news in a simple and convenient format.

The app collects and displays news content from third-party news providers through authorized APIs. Wherever available, source and publisher information is displayed to ensure transparency and proper attribution.

Thuthar AI also provides AI-powered news summaries and translation features to help users quickly understand important stories.

Developer: Isaac Darlong

Thuthar AI is committed to providing an informative and user-friendly news experience while respecting the intellectual property rights of content publishers.
